GeoUI Intent Protocol schemas

JSON Schemas of the GeoUI Intent Protocol. Each extension URI below is the address of its own schema.json; a published version never changes.

Core document: https://nasa-impact.github.io/geoui-intent-protocol/geo_intent.json

Extensions

  • https://nasa-impact.github.io/geoui-intent-protocol/ext/compare/v1.0.0/schema.json — Compare two layer stacks or two dates side by side (swipe, spy or opacity blend).
  • https://nasa-impact.github.io/geoui-intent-protocol/ext/chart/v1.0.0/schema.json — Open a time-series chart of one layer over an area of interest and a date range.
  • https://nasa-impact.github.io/geoui-intent-protocol/ext/raster-styling/v1.0.0/schema.json — Restyle a raster layer: palette, style, value range and squash. Fields live on each LayerRef.
